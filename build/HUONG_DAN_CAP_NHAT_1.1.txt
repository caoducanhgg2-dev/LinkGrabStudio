LINKGRAB STUDIO 1.1 - GOI CAP NHAT NHO

1. Dong hoan toan LinkGrab Studio.
2. Giai nen TOAN BO file ZIP vao mot thu muc rieng.
3. Chay CapNhat_1.1.cmd.
4. Neu ung dung dang nam o vi tri khac, keo thu muc cai dat vao file CMD
   hoac chay: CapNhat_1.1.cmd "D:\duong-dan\LinkGrabStudio"

Trinh cap nhat chi thay file chuong trinh da thay doi.
Lich su tai va danh sach chong trung trong LOCALAPPDATA duoc giu nguyen.
Truoc khi thay file, ban cu duoc sao luu. Neu co loi, ban cu tu dong duoc khoi phuc.
